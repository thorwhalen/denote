denote.keys

denote.keys¶

Key estimation from pitch-class profiles (the Krumhansl-Schmuckler method).

Correlate a 12-bin pitch-class vector (the mean chroma of a recording, or a note histogram of a score) with a major and a minor key profile rotated to each of the 12 tonics. The best of the 24 Pearson correlations names the key. This module is the audio-free core; denote.get_key computes the chroma and calls it.

>>> major = KEY_PROFILES["krumhansl"].major
>>> r = key_from_chroma(major)  # the C major profile itself
>>> r.key, round(r.confidence, 3)
('C major', 1.0)
>>> r.relative
'A minor'
>>> key_from_chroma(np.roll(major, 3)).key  # rotated up three semitones
'Eb major'
>>> parse_key("F# minor")
(6, 'minor')

Profiles (KEY_PROFILES), each a published table of how well each pitch class fits a key, tonic first:

  • "krumhansl": Krumhansl and Kessler’s probe-tone ratings (Krumhansl & Kessler 1982; Krumhansl, Cognitive Foundations of Musical Pitch, 1990, Table 2.1).

  • "temperley": Temperley’s profiles from the Kostka-Payne corpus (Temperley, Music and Probability, 2007), as listed by Humdrum keycor.

Both sets of numbers are as given at https://extras.humdrum.org/man/keycor/ (and in music21’s analysis.discrete). Any other (major, minor) pair of 12 numbers can be passed as profile.

Key names follow the fewest-accidentals convention (Eb major, C# minor, Gb major, Eb minor), the one chordsym uses for chord spelling.

denote.keys.key_from_chroma(chroma, *, profile='krumhansl', weights=None, backend='')[source]¶

The best of the 24 keys for a chroma vector or chromagram.

Parameters:

  • chroma – (12,) pitch-class weights (C first) or a (12, frames) chromagram, averaged over frames.

  • profile (Union[str, KeyProfile, Tuple[Sequence[float], Sequence[float]]]) – A name in KEY_PROFILES (default "krumhansl"), a KeyProfile, or a (major, minor) pair of 12 weights each.

  • weights – Optional per-frame weights for the average (e.g. frame RMS).

  • backend (str) – Recorded on the result.

Return type:

KeyResult

Returns:

KeyResult whose confidence is the winner’s correlation, margin the gap to the runner-up, scores every key’s correlation (best first), and raw["chroma"] the 12-bin vector correlated.

denote.keys.key_scores(chroma, *, profile='krumhansl')[source]¶

{key name: Pearson correlation} for all 24 keys, best first.

Raises ValueError when the vector is flat to within FLAT_RTOL (silence, or no pitched content), since a correlation with a constant is undefined. Level gating (very quiet or very short audio) is the audio adapter’s job: per-frame chroma normalization makes even faint noise look pitched here.

Return type:

Dict[str, float]

denote.keys.parse_key(name)[source]¶

(tonic_pc, mode) from a key name.

Accepted: "Eb major", "c# minor", "A min", "Am", "Amin", "Amaj" and a bare uppercase tonic ("Bb", major). A bare lowercase tonic ("a") is rejected, because some conventions read it as minor; say "a minor" or "am".

>>> parse_key("Eb major"), parse_key("Am"), parse_key("Bbmaj")
((3, 'major'), (9, 'minor'), (10, 'major'))
Return type:

Tuple[int, str]
